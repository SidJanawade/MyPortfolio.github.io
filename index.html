I'm an AI and backend engineer based in Pune. I graduated in 2025 with a B.Tech in Computer Science from SRMIST, Chennai, and spent my first role at Hitachi Solutions building the kind of AI systems that real teams depend on every day.

What I care about most is measuring quality. It's easy to get a demo working; it's harder to know whether a retrieval system is getting better or quietly regressing. So I build evaluation into everything: metrics like Hit@3 and MRR, quality gates in CI, and production monitoring.

I work mainly in Python and FastAPI for retrieval and LLM services, and I enjoy going lower level too. I wrote a lock-free C++20 queue and benchmarked it down to p99.9 latency, because understanding performance from the ground up makes me a better backend engineer.
